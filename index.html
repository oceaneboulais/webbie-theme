Science with Teeth

Every illustration starts with scientific research — morphological structure, ecological significance, and biogeography — then gets dialed up to eleven.

No polar bears giving piggyback rides to penguins here. Just hand-inked oceanographic fauna that could hang in a lab, a venue, or both.

Sustainably Produced

  • Blanks chosen for organic content, ethical labour certification, and durability.
  • Produced in small batches locally in San Diego, CA.
  • Exclusive use of Algae-Ink's carbon-negative pigments.
  • Packaging is compostable. No single-use plastic in the supply chain.
